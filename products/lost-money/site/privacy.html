Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Lost Money Playbook ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses and protects your information when you visit our website, take our quiz, buy our products or receive our emails. We keep this simple and in plain English.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you take our quiz, we ask for your first name and email address. We also record your quiz answers.
  • Purchase information. Purchases are processed by Hotmart, our payment and delivery platform. Hotmart collects your payment details. We do not see or store your full card number. Hotmart shares with us basic order details such as your name, email, the product you bought and the order status.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we and our partners collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device, pages visited and how you arrived at our site. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies (see section 4).

We do not ask for your Social Security number, bank account details or any information needed to file an unclaimed property claim. Official searches and claims are done directly on official government websites, not through us.

2. How we use your information

  • To show you your quiz result and deliver the free tips you requested.
  • To deliver the products you buy and provide customer support.
  • To send you emails about our guide, related products and helpful tips (see section 5).
  • To measure and improve our website, ads and products.
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as:

  • Hotmart, for payment processing, product delivery and refunds.
  • Our email service provider, to send the emails you signed up for.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram), for advertising measurement through the Meta Pixel.
  • Hosting and analytics providers, to run and improve our website.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, or as part of a sale or transfer of our business.

4. Cookies and the Meta Pixel

We use cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel. The Meta Pixel helps us understand which ads lead people to our site and to purchases, and helps Meta show our ads to people who may be interested. Meta may collect information about your visit (such as pages viewed and actions taken) and combine it with other information it holds, under Meta's own privacy policy.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account settings. Blocking cookies may affect some parts of the site.

5. Email marketing and unsubscribing

When you give us your email, you agree to receive emails from us, including your quiz result, tips and offers related to our products. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. Click it at any time and we will stop sending you marketing emails. If you bought a product, we may still send you messages about your order.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as you are on our email list or as needed to provide our products, handle refunds and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. After that, we delete or anonymize it.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it for marketing. Depending on where you live (for example, California and other U.S. states with privacy laws), you may have additional rights, including the right to know what we collect and to opt out of certain sharing for advertising. To make a request, email us at the address below. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Our website and products are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Not a government website

The Lost Money Playbook is an independent publisher. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government agency, state unclaimed property program, NAUPA or MissingMoney.com.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Please check this page periodically.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Contact us through your Hotmart purchase receipt (open the purchase email from Hotmart and use "Contact the producer" / Help).
