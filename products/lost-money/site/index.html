Why money gets "lost" (and goes to the state)

When a bank, employer, insurer or utility can't reach you, the law requires them to hand that money to the state. The state keeps it, sometimes for decades, until you or your heirs claim it. Nobody calls you. Nobody sends a letter to your new address. You have to go look.

The problem: most people search wrong

They type their name into one website, see nothing, and give up. But money hides in other states, under a misspelled name, under a maiden name, under a parent who passed away, or in federal programs that the state sites don't show at all.
