# Script guide — True Crime & Mysteries channel (English)

Reference: the approved PT-BR sample `dbcooper-amostra/` (owner: "ficou perfeito"). Same tone, in English.

## Format
- Length: **2,800–3,600 words of narration** (≈ 19–24 min at ~150 wpm).
- Language: natural, cinematic American English. Short sentences. Rhythm. Ellipses for dramatic pauses ("...").
- Narrator voice: calm, grave, investigative (ElevenLabs documentary voice). Never shouty. Tension comes from rhythm and withheld information.
- ElevenLabs audio tags allowed sparingly: `[whispers]`, `[pause]` (max ~5 per script).

## Structure (retention)
1. **Cold open (0:00–0:45)**: drop into the most dramatic moment, end with the central question. No "welcome to the channel".
2. **Promise (0:45–1:10)**: what the viewer will understand by the end ("By the end of this video, you'll know...").
3. **Chapters (4–6)** with on-screen titles. Each chapter ends on an open loop / re-hook. Re-hook every 2–3 minutes.
4. **The twist / deepest layer** in the last third (theory, new evidence, irony).
5. **Ending**: resolve what can be resolved, honestly state what remains unknown, a final haunting line. One short CTA at most (subscribe for the next case), last 10 seconds.

## Accuracy & safety rules (non-negotiable)
- Only well-documented facts. When unsure of a number/date/name, write it and mark **[VERIFY]** right after it.
- People never convicted = "suspect", "person of interest", "alleged". Present theories as theories.
- **No gore.** No graphic description of injuries or bodies. Violence is implied, never detailed.
- Respect victims and families. No mocking. No speculation about private individuals that could be defamatory.
- Don't invent quotes. If a quote is paraphrased, say "reportedly" / "according to".
